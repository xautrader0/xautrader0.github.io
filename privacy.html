MINI JARVIS

Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Mini JARVIS is a private desktop application used only by its owner, on the owner's own Windows PC, to view statistics for the owner's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data it accesses and what happens to it.

Data accessed

Only after the account owner signs in through the platform's official sign-in page and approves read-only access:

  • YouTube (Google): channel information, video list and video statistics, and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, average view duration, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained and lost). If the owner chooses, also YouTube revenue reports (estimated revenue, RPM). Scopes: youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly and optionally yt-analytics-monetary.readonly.
  • TikTok: basic profile information, profile statistics (followers, likes, video count) and the list of public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts. Scopes: user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): for one Instagram professional account and its linked Facebook Page chosen by the owner: account and Page insights, the list of posts and their insights.

Mini JARVIS never asks for, sees or stores account passwords.

How the data is used

Solely to show the owner the performance of their own accounts in the Mini JARVIS dashboard on their PC, and to compare performance across their projects. The data is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used to train any AI model.

Where the data is stored

All data stays on the owner's PC, in Mini JARVIS's local database. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) and are never displayed or written to logs. Mini JARVIS has no server of its own; it communicates only with the platforms' official APIs.

Retention and deletion

  • Statistics are kept until the owner deletes them.
  • Disconnect in Mini JARVIS revokes the access token with the platform and deletes the stored tokens. It can also delete all statistics collected for that account.
  • Access can also be removed at any time from the platform itself: Google account permissions, TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, Facebook → Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites.

YouTube API Services

Mini JARVIS uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Mini JARVIS's access to your data via the Google security settings page.

Mini JARVIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Mini JARVIS is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.

Changes and contact
